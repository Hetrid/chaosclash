GOAL
Last hero standing wins the round. First to the round target (default 3) wins the match. A storm closes in from the edges, so keep moving toward the safe zone shown on the minimap.
DESKTOP CONTROLS
WASD / Arrows move · Hold left mouse shoot · Right mouse or Q / Space first ability · E second ability · R ultimate · Shift dodge (a dodge timed with an incoming shot is a Perfect Dodge: cooldowns refund and a speed burst) · G / middle mouse ping · Tab scoreboard · M mute · F fps
MOBILE CONTROLS
Left stick moves, right stick aims and auto-fires. ATTACK locks onto the nearest enemy. Tap an ability to auto-aim it, or press and drag to place it, then release. Drag to the ✕ to cancel. The pencil button lets you rearrange buttons.
SYSTEMS
Bushes hide you from anyone outside them until you shoot or take damage. Cover blocks shots and can be destroyed. Frozen targets take 1.5× damage. Orbs restore HP or shield, boost speed or damage, or refresh cooldowns. Passives (two per hero) are picked in the PASSIVES tab.
SOLO PRACTICE
Bots use their hero's abilities, dodge, strafe, grab orbs and rotate with the storm. Set bot count, difficulty and map in the PRACTICE tab. Your career record on the main menu is saved in this browser.
