LEGEND ARENA

Sundered Vale · 5v5 · Three Lanes

Original game · no affiliation with any existing MOBA

HOW TO PLAY

Goal: destroy the enemy Core before yours falls. There is no match timer — games end only by Core destruction or surrender.

Left joystick moves your hero. Right buttons: Basic attack, Skill 1, Skill 2, Ultimate, Battle Spell, Recall.

Tap a skill for a quick cast (auto-aim assist). Drag from a skill to aim precisely. Drag into the ✕ zone to cancel.

Target lock: the small HERO / MINION / TURRET buttons under the basic-attack button set your auto-attack priority.

Farm minions for gold (last hits pay best), take jungle camps, slay the Ancient Shell then the War Colossus, push lanes, win.

Shop opens automatically in fountain (or tap the gold button). Recall channels back to base — damage or moving cancels it.

Junglers carry Hunt (executes monsters). Roam builds a Roam Compass income item instead of farming.

Desktop testing: WASD move, Q / E / R skills, Space basic, B recall, F spell, P shop, Tab scoreboard.
